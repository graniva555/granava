Care & Maintenance

01

Daily Cleaning

Use a microfibre cloth with warm water and pH-neutral soap. Buff dry to remove fingerprints from the high-polish surface.

02

Sealing

Apply an impregnating sealer every 12 months to maintain reflective depth.

03

Stain Removal

Blot spills immediately. Use a stone-specific poultice rather than scrubbing.

04

What to Avoid

Never use acidic or ammonia-based cleaners — they dull the pearl sheen.

Frequently Asked Questions

What gives Black Pearl its shimmer?
Labradorite and feldspar crystals create a shifting metallic lustre — most pronounced on the high-polish finish.
Is Black Pearl the same as Black Galaxy?
No. Black Galaxy has golden flecks; Black Pearl has a uniform pearl sheen without large flecks.
Which finish best shows the pearl effect?
The High Polish finish maximises the pearl shimmer.
What is the minimum order quantity?
80 square metres.
