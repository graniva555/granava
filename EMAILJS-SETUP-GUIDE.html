Granava — EmailJS Setup Guide

This connects your contact form so inquiries arrive in your inbox. Takes about 10 minutes. EmailJS is free for up to 200 emails/month.

Part 1 — Create your EmailJS account

1Go to emailjs.com and sign up (free).
2After login you land on the Dashboard.

Part 2 — Connect your email (the "Service")

1Left menu → Email Services → Add New Service.
2Choose your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, or your info@granava.in host). For Gmail, click Connect Account and authorize.
3Once created, copy the Service ID — it looks like service_abc123. Save it.

Part 3 — Create the notification template (email TO you)

1Left menu → Email Templates → Create New Template.
2Set To Email to info@granava.in, and Subject to {{subject}}.
3Paste this into the template body (HTML editor):
New granite inquiry from the Granava website

Name:        {{from_name}}
Company:     {{company_name}}
Email:       {{reply_to}}
Phone:       {{phone}}

Products:    {{products_interest}}
Application: {{application_type}}
Quantity:    {{quantity}}
Delivery to: {{delivery_country}}

Message:
{{message}}
4Set Reply To field to {{reply_to}} so you can reply directly to the customer.
5Save. Copy this template's Template ID — looks like template_xyz789.
Important: The variable names in double braces (like {{from_name}}) must match exactly — they're listed in the table below. Don't rename them.

Part 4 — (Optional) Auto-reply template (email TO the customer)

This sends an automatic "we received your inquiry" email to whoever filled the form.

1Create another template. Set To Email to {{reply_to}}.
2Subject: Thank you for contacting Granava. Body:
Dear {{from_name}},

Thank you for your interest in Granava natural granite.
We've received your inquiry about {{products_interest}} and
our export team will respond within 1 business day.

Warm regards,
The Granava Team
info@granava.in
3Save and copy this Template ID too (this is your "Reply Template ID").

Part 5 — Get your Public Key

1Left menu → Account → General.
2Copy your Public Key — looks like aB3dEf7GhiJkLmno.

Part 6 — Put your 4 values into the website

Open index.html in a text editor. Search for YOUR_SERVICE_ID. You'll find these four lines near the contact form:

const SERVICE_ID        = 'YOUR_SERVICE_ID';
const TEMPLATE_ID       = 'YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID';
const REPLY_TEMPLATE_ID = 'YOUR_REPLY_TEMPLATE_ID';
const PUBLIC_KEY        = 'YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY';

Replace each placeholder with your real value, for example:

const SERVICE_ID        = 'service_abc123';
const TEMPLATE_ID       = 'template_xyz789';
const REPLY_TEMPLATE_ID = 'template_reply55';
const PUBLIC_KEY        = 'aB3dEf7GhiJkLmno';
If you're not using the auto-reply, just set REPLY_TEMPLATE_ID to the same value as TEMPLATE_ID — it won't break anything.

Part 7 — Save, upload, test

1Save index.html and upload it to your GitHub repo (replace the old one).
2Wait ~1 minute for the site to redeploy.
3Open your live site, fill the contact form, and submit. Check your info@granava.in inbox — the inquiry should arrive within seconds.

Template variable reference

These are the exact variables the form sends. Use them in your EmailJS templates:

VariableWhat it contains
{{from_name}}Customer's full name
{{company_name}}Their company (or "Not provided")
{{reply_to}}Customer's email (use in Reply-To)
{{to_email}}info@granava.in
{{phone}}Country code + phone number
{{products_interest}}Granites they're interested in
{{application_type}}Countertops, flooring, etc.
{{quantity}}Quantity needed
{{delivery_country}}Destination country
{{message}}Their free-text message
{{subject}}Auto-generated email subject line
Troubleshooting: If emails don't arrive — (1) check the browser console (F12) for an "EmailJS error", (2) verify all 4 IDs are correct with no extra spaces, (3) check your EmailJS dashboard "Email History" tab to see if the send was attempted, (4) check spam folder.
